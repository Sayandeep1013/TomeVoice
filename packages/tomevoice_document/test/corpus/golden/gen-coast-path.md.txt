## A Week on the Coast Path
A Week on the Coast Path
Notes written up each evening, in the order the days came.
## Day One: Lantern Cove
Day One: Lantern Cove
We left the station at nine and were on the cliffs by ten.
The path is well marked, with a white acorn on every gate, and the first miles are easy going.
By noon we could see Lantern Cove below us, its harbour wall curled like a hand around six small boats.
“Mind the tide,” said the woman at the café.
“It comes in faster than you think, and it doesn’t wait for walkers.”
What we carried:
a map, a compass and the tide tables;
two litres of water each;
far too many sandwiches.
## Day Two: The Salt Road
Day Two: The Salt Road
The second day was longer.
We followed the old salt road inland, past the ruined chapel and over the moor, and came down to the sea again at dusk.
Distances, from the guidebook:
Stage Miles Climb (ft)
Lantern Cove 4 350
The Salt Road 9 780
Gull Point 6 420
The guidebook’s website has the full list.
Its advice for the moor is short:
Keep the wall on your left.
Do not trust the cairns.
If the mist comes down, stop.
## Day Three: Gull Point
Day Three: Gull Point
We woke to rain and walked out of it by eleven.
The last stretch to Gull Point is the finest on the whole path: high, bare and loud with birds.
We sat on the headland until the light went, and nobody said much.
