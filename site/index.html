A closer look

Your shelves, the page as it's read, a web novel of three thousand chapters, and colours of your own.

What it does

Everything happens on the phone. Nothing you read is sent anywhere, unless you turn on the AI companion below.

Opens almost anything

  • Books in any format. EPUB, PDF, DjVu, DOCX, ODT, MOBI and AZW3, FB2, RTF, HTML, Markdown and plain text.
  • Scanned PDFs. Read by OCR on the phone, in the languages you choose.
  • Web pages. Share a link and it reads the article, without menus or ads.
  • Web novels. Search, follow and download from novel sites, with new chapters found for you.
  • Catalogues. Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, or your own Calibre library.

Reads it to you

  • Your phone's voices. A voice in the book's language, picked for you.
  • Natural voices. About 180 that sound like people, in 42 languages, made on the phone with no internet.
  • Dialogue and characters. Speech in a second voice, or each character in a voice of their own.
  • Screen off. Lock screen, headset and Android Auto controls; it stops for calls.
  • Save as audio. A chapter, or the whole book as an M4B or MP3 audiobook.

Made for reading too

  • Pages or a scroll. Pages curl like paper; on its side the book lies open.
  • Papers and themes. Ink, Papyrus, Dusk, colours from covers, or your own.
  • A reading journal. Write, or speak, what you think as you read, each entry stamped with where the story had reached.
  • Quotes and bookmarks. Keep a sentence, or share it as a picture in the book's colours.
  • Backups and sync. Automatic backups to a folder, and sync to your own server if you want it.

A companion that remembers the book

New in 2.0, and off until you want it: an AI that reads along with you, using a key of your own from Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, OpenAI or another service. There's no TomeVoice server in between.

It remembers

  • A memory of each book. Who everyone is, what happened and why, what's still unanswered, chapter by chapter.
  • Who's who. Hold a name for their card at once, even three thousand chapters in.
  • The memory graph. The story's web of people, who matters now and who you may have forgotten; its threads, step by step.

Talk about it

  • Ask anything. Answers cite the chapters they come from, and are read aloud in the book's voice.
  • Explain this. Hold a sentence to hear what it means there in the story.
  • Previously… Back after a few days, a short recap of where you were.

On your terms

  • No spoilers. Turn on the guard and it keeps to what you've read.
  • Your theories. Say what you think is going on; it tells you when the story settles it, never before.
  • Kept private. Any book can be kept away from the AI, and secret shelves always are.

Install it in a minute

TomeVoice isn't on the Play Store. It's installed from its own file, which Android lets you do once you allow it.

  1. Download the app. Tap Download above on your phone. Most phones take the main file; very old ones need the 32-bit one.
  2. Allow the install. Open the file. If Android asks, let your browser install apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open a book. Add a file from your phone, share a link, or start with Pride and Prejudice, already on the shelf.

Updates by themselves. Add TomeVoice to Obtainium with this address, and it tells you when a new release is out: https://github.com/Sayandeep1013/TomeVoice
