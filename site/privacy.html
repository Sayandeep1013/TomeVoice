TomeVoice

Privacy

Effective 20 November 2026 · applies to TomeVoice for Android, from Google Play and from GitHub

In short: TomeVoice has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no server of its own. Your books, places, notes and journal stay on your phone.

Something leaves the phone only when you use a feature that needs it: a voice or language download, a catalogue you browse, a link you open, a sync server you set up, the AI with a key you add, or a report you choose to send. Each is listed below.

Who we are

TomeVoice is free, open-source software (GPL-3.0), published on Google Play by Nofilterr Agency and on GitHub as Sayandeep1013/TomeVoice. For any question about this policy, write to nofilterr.agency@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/Sayandeep1013/TomeVoice/issues.

What stays on your phone

  • Your books and everything about them: where you are, bookmarks, quotes, reading time, covers, settings.
  • Your journal, and the talks with the AI that are kept in it.
  • Each book's AI memory (its notes, cast, graph, threads and theories), built from the AI's answers.
  • Your AI keys and server passwords. They are kept in the app's own storage and are never put in a backup: not in TomeVoice's, and not in Android's backup to Google.
  • A list of the calls made to the AI (when, for what, which book, which model, how many tokens), so you can see what was sent. It never leaves the phone.
  • A log of the app's own errors, with book titles and personal details taken out. It is sent only if you choose "Report a problem", which opens a GitHub issue you can read before sending.

The AI

  • Nothing is sent to any AI until you add a key and agree, in the app, to what is sent.
  • The key is yours, and the provider is the one you pick. TomeVoice never sees what you send or what comes back: it goes straight between your phone and that provider. What the provider keeps, and whether it learns from it, is in its own terms (Google's free Gemini tier, for one, may use requests to improve its models).
  • Books on a secret shelf, and books you keep away from the AI, are never sent.
  • AI answers can be wrong or unsuitable. Any answer can be reported from the Talk screen; it is then hidden.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in or account, and no advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data: the developer receives nothing from your phone except an AI answer you report, or a problem report you send.
  • No location, contacts, photos or files beyond the books you choose to open.

Keeping it safe, and taking it away

  • Everything sent over the internet goes over HTTPS, except a server you set up yourself with a plain http:// address (sync warns you before sending a password to one).
  • You can see what was sent to the AI (AI › What was sent), export every book's memory (AI › Export every memory), and forget one memory or all of them. Uninstalling TomeVoice removes everything it kept on the phone.
  • A report you sent can be removed: ask through the GitHub issues link above.

Children

TomeVoice is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new one is posted here with its date, and the app's release notes say so.
