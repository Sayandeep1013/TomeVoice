# 2.0 test list: what was added, and what to check on the phone

This list is kept up to date as 2.0 is built (branch `ai-companion`). Each item says what was added and how to check it. Tick items off as you go, and tell me what looks or feels wrong.

**Before you start:** install the test app (TomeVoice Test) from the latest `ai-companion` build, and have a key ready: Mercury (Inception), Gemini, or both.

---

## A. Fixes from 19 Nov (also in 2.0)

1. **PDF pages hide the bars.** In a PDF shown as its real pages, a tap on a page hides the top bar and the player; another tap brings them back. Check scrolled pages, turned pages, and a double tap to zoom (that should zoom only, with no flicker of the bars).
2. **Talk and the pen, at the right.** Both are round buttons at the right, in line with the player's edge: upright, Talk above the pen; on its side, side by side. They no longer push the player's controls aside, and they come and go with the bars. *(Was: the pen alone, and earlier Talk at the left.)*

   **The pen's own button.** The pen sits in a round button just above the player, at the right, and no longer pushes the player's controls aside. It comes and goes with the bars.
3. **PDF import keeps its frames.** Import a big PDF: the library should stay smooth while "Reading page N of M…" counts up.

## B. Keys (Main menu → AI)

4. **Add keys.** Add your Mercury key: pick "Inception (Mercury)", paste the key, then tap **Check the key**. It should say how many models there are and fill in `mercury-3` for both jobs. Save.
5. **Several keys from one provider.** Add a second (and third) Mercury key, and Gemini keys if you have them. Each gets its own name ("Inception (Mercury) 2"…). Nothing stops you adding more.
6. **Job order.** Under "Building memories" and "Talking", hold a key and drag it to change the order.
7. **Key states.** A key whose quota is used shows "Resting until 05:30"; a wrong key shows "Refused: …". Each key shows today's calls and tokens.
8. **Keys never shown whole.** Only the last four characters show (••••abcd).

## C. A book's memory (book page → Memory, once a key exists)

9. **The Memory row** on a book's page is one line ("Built to chapter 30 · 18 people, places and things", with a thin bar while building); a tap opens the book's Memory page with everything below. It (and a web novel's page) only after a key is added, and never for a book on a secret shelf.
10. **Build memory.** Tap Build memory. It first indexes the words (free, on the phone), then shows an estimate: chapters, calls, tokens and which keys. Tap Build.
    - Watch "Building · N of M chapters" and the bar move.
    - Leave the page and come back: it should still be going.
11. **Pause** stops after the batch in flight; **Go on building** resumes.
12. **Resting.** When every key's quota is used, it says "Waiting… Goes on at HH:MM" and goes on by itself then (while the app is open).
13. **Spoiler guard** switch, off by default. On, the AI keeps to what you've read.
14. **Forget this memory** (⋮ next to the title) deletes it; your journal stays.
15. **Web novels.** Only the chapters downloaded are indexed and built. Download more and build again to add them.

## D. Talk (in the reader)

16. **The Talk button.** It sits at the left, just above the player (the pen is at the right), shows only once a key exists, and hides with the bars on a tap.
17. **Asking.** Tap Talk. A half-height sheet opens over the page, with the voice still reading.
    - Try the suggested questions, then your own.
    - Answers carry small chapter chips ("ch 1204"). Tap one: the page shows that place, the voice reads on, and "Back to reading" returns.
18. **Guard on.** With the guard on, the sheet says "Keeping to chapter N". Ask about something later in the book: it should not tell you.
19. **Talks are kept.** Close the sheet, open the Journal: the talk is there as "A talk up to chapter N", with ✦.
    - The filter **All · My writing · Talks** shows only the chosen kind.
    - Tap a talk to read it again.
    - Export includes talks.

## E. Holding a sentence (in the reader)

20. **Explain this** (at the top of the menu, once a key exists) opens Talk already asking what the sentence means there.
21. **Who or what is "word"?** Hold a name (Sunny, Nephis, a place, a power). The card opens straight away, with no waiting for the AI: who or what it is, other names, how it stands (rank, place…), relations, key events by chapter, and a line they said.
    - Tap a line to look at its chapter.
    - **Tell me more** asks Talk.

## F. The story so far

22. **Summaries.** After 25 chapters have notes, the Builder also writes a summary of those 25 (and of every 250). Ask Talk "Catch me up on the story so far": the answer should draw on them.

## G. Who's who and the memory graph (book page → Memory, once it has people)

23. **Who's who** lists everyone and everything the memory knows, with a chip for each kind ("People 212", "Places 40", "Aspects 15"…).
    - Search by any name.
    - Sort by Seen most, First met or By name.
    - Tap one: their card.
    - With the guard on, only what you've reached shows.
24. **The graph** (Memory page → Graph, or the button in Who's who), rebuilt from Obsidian's, Kumu's and the reading companions' maps:
    - It opens **round the main character**: Round Mira · Two links out · The whole web. "Minor ones" shows those met only once.
    - 🔍 finds anyone by any name and centres the web on them; the fit button brings it back to the screen.
    - Lines are coloured by what they are (allies green, enemies red, family blue, belonging purple and dashed, shared events grey), with a legend. Groups wear a soft halo round their members.
    - **Timeline:** the slider shows the web at any chapter, and nobody moves. ▶ plays it growing from chapter 1, and newcomers glow.
    - Tap someone: their links light up, the rest dims, and a panel shows what they are as of that chapter, a strip of the chapters they're in, and their relations as chips (tap to go there), plus **Card**, **Centre here** and **Linked with…** (then tap a second person: their relations and the events they shared, by chapter).
    - Names sit on small pills and never overlap; the important ones win.

## G2. Journal on the book's page, and in your theme

- The Journal row on a book's page is one line: how many entries, the latest and its first words, a ✎ to write. A tap opens that book's journal.
- The journal's notebook look (list, pages, writing page) takes your colour theme's colours, not only plain dark or white. Check a couple of themes in Settings.
- **Main menu → Memory** (below AI): every book's memory; each opens its Memory page.

## H. Theories

25. **State a theory.** In Talk, tap the bulb left of the input (or the "I have a theory…" chip) and say what you think is going on. You see "My theory: …", and the AI weighs it: what supports it, what's against it (cited), what's unknown, and how likely it is.
26. **Theories list** (book page → Memory → Theories): every theory you've stated, with **Open / Confirmed / Busted / Partly right**. Hold one to remove it.
27. **Settled as you read.** As the Builder makes notes for later chapters, it checks your open theories.
    - Once you **reach** the chapter that settled one, opening the book says "Your theory from chapter 900 was right: chapter 1450 settled it", with a **See** button.
    - You're never told before you get there, even with the guard off.

## I. Answers aloud

28. **Spoken in your reading voice.** While the book is reading, ask in Talk:
    - The book pauses, and the answer is said in the same voice (a sentence or so at a time).
    - The book reads on from where it was once the answer is done.
    - Close the sheet mid-answer: the answer stops and the book reads on.
29. **The speaker button** at the top right of the sheet turns answers aloud on or off (remembered). **Say it again** under an answer repeats it.
30. **Natural voices.** Check with a natural voice and with a phone (Google) voice.

## J. Kept up as you read, Previously…, Talk settings

31. **Kept up on its own.** For a book whose memory has notes, new chapters get notes without you tapping anything:
    - when you open the book, and at each chapter's end;
    - for a web novel, 20 seconds after new chapters download.

    A book you never built is left alone, and nothing starts while another book's memory is building.
32. **Previously…** Open a book you haven't read for 3+ days (and whose memory has notes): a note offers "Previously…", which opens Talk with a short recap of the chapters just before your place.
33. **AI → Talk settings:**
    - Answers aloud.
    - How long answers are (Short / Normal / Long).
    - "Previously…" after Never / a day / 3 days / a week.
    - "What the AI already knows of the book": off by default. Turned on, a well-known book may get facts from the model's own memory, and it says so.

## K. A web novel's whole memory (Shadow Slave)

34. **Download first.** On a web novel with chapters not on the phone, Build memory first says "N chapters are not on the phone", with two choices:
    - **Download them, and build:** they go to the usual download queue, one at a time.
    - **Build with what is here.**
35. **Joining as they come.** While they download, every 20 seconds or so the new chapters are indexed and noted. Watch the count on the Memory section climb. Your keys rest and wake as their quotas run out and come back.

## L. Building with TomeVoice closed

36. **AI → Building memories:**
    - "Also with TomeVoice closed" (on by default).
    - "Only on Wi-Fi" (on).
    - "Only while charging" (off).
37. **Closed-app check.** Start a big build (Shadow Slave), then close TomeVoice fully (swipe it away). Come back after an hour or two on Wi-Fi: the count on its Memory section should have moved on.
    - It never runs while TomeVoice is open; the app builds itself then.
    - It never runs alongside the new-chapter check.

## M. Talk elsewhere

38. **Book page → Memory → Talk elsewhere:**
    - **Copy:** paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or NotebookLM.
    - **Share as a file:** a `.md` file.

    It holds the story so far, who matters (with how they stand and their relations), what's still unanswered, your theories and your journal. With the guard on, it stops at your chapter and asks the other AI not to spoil past it.

## N. The AI page's Memories, and series

39. **AI → Memories:** every book with a memory, how far it's built (with a bar), and a Pause or Go on button. Watch Shadow Slave here.
40. **Series** (book page → Memory → ⋮ → "Follows on from another book…"): pick the book before it. Its people, places and things, how they stood, open threads and story come over.
    - Who's who shows them as "from an earlier book".
    - Talk knows them from the start.

## O. Keeping a book away from the AI

41. **Book page → Memory → ⋮ → "Keep this book away from the AI":**
    - The section says so, and its buttons go.
    - The reader's Talk button and the Explain/Who menu items go.
    - Nothing is built for it in the background.
    - "Let the AI read this book" undoes it.

## P. Meaning search (with a Gemini key)

42. **Setup.** On a Gemini key (AI → the key), fill "Meaning search" with its embedding model ("Check the key" suggests one). As chapters are noted, each also gets a vector.
43. **Asking in other words.** In Talk, describe a moment without its words ("the time he nearly froze to death"). The answer should find it and cite the chapter, even though the text never says "froze to death".

## Q. Read elsewhere first

44. **Book page → Memory → ⋮ → "I've read up to chapter…":** type 1800 for a book you'd read that far elsewhere. With the spoiler guard on, the AI keeps to chapter 1800 (or your place here, if that's further). Leave it empty to go by your place alone.
